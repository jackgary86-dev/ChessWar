# Demo day script

A 5-minute walkthrough for showing Chess War to friends. One laptop, one browser tab, sound on.

**Set up beforehand (2 minutes).** Open the game in a fresh tab. Once the `?demo` build is merged, use `<address>/?demo`: it is vs AI only, five rounds, with on-screen hints for the first round. Otherwise choose "Play vs AI". Check the mute button, zoom the browser so the back row can read the board, and have a second person ready to say the pitch line.

## Pitch line (15 seconds)

> "It's an auto-battler played with real chess pieces. You buy pieces, place them on your half of a split board, and then they fight on their own. Three of the same piece merge into a stronger one."

## Live round (about 3 minutes)

| Time | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:15 | Start the game. Point at the shop. | "Five cards, each costs gold. Pawns are cheap, the queen costs 5." |
| 0:45 | Buy two or three cheap pieces. | "Pieces are pooled, so if I buy them, my opponent cannot." |
| 1:15 | Tap a bench piece, then a green square. Then drag one. | "Tap or drag. I can only place on my own half." |
| 1:45 | Press Fight. Use 2× speed. | "Now nobody touches anything. Pieces move like chess pieces but trade damage instead of capturing at once." |
| 2:15 | Point at a piece crossing a portal. | "The wall blocks everyone except through the glowing portal squares. Knights leap it." |
| 2:45 | Read the result screen. | "The loser takes damage from the winner's surviving pieces. First to 0 HP loses." |

## Key mechanics to point out

1. **Portals.** Only crossing points are the glowing squares (ranks 6 and 3). Knights jump the wall.
2. **Merging.** Three copies make a ★★, three ★★ make a ★★★. Merge bursts play on the board or bench.
3. **Abilities at ★★ and ★★★.** Pawn Shield Wall, Knight Fork, Bishop Blessing, Rook Fortress, Queen Pierce. The Field Manual lists them.
4. **Economy.** Interest on saved gold (1 per 10, max 5), win and loss streaks, level ups that raise the board cap to 8.
5. **Modes.** Hot-seat (handoff screen hides each board) and online 1v1 with a room code.

## If something goes wrong

- Screen is blank or art is missing: reload; pieces fall back to glyphs and stay playable.
- Fight drags on: press "Skip to result".
- Wrong click on the shop: select the piece and use Sell for a refund.
