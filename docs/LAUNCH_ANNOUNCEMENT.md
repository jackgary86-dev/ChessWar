## Game Portal listing

**Title:** Chess War

**One line:** An auto-battler played with real chess pieces.

**Description:**
Two armies, two boards, one wall. Buy pieces from the shop, merge three copies into a stronger star level, and place up to eight on your board. Then watch the fight play out on its own: pieces move like chess pieces, but they trade blows with HP and damage instead of instant captures. Cross the wall only through the glowing portal squares (knights can leap it anywhere). Last player with HP wins the war.

**Modes:** vs AI, two players on one screen (hot-seat), and online 1v1 with a room code.
