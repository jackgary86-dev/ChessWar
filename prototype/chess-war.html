CHESS WAR

Two boards, two armies. Buy pieces, stack copies into stars, place up to 8 on your board, and watch them fight it out.

  • Shop phase. Spend gold on pieces, rerolls and XP. Your level sets how many pieces you can field, up to 8.
  • Fight. Both armies are revealed and battle automatically with real chess movement. Damage replaces instant captures.
  • War. Losing a fight costs HP. First commander at 0 HP loses.
