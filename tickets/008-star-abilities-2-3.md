# Star abilities (2★ / 3★)

**Milestone:** M1 Game logic core  
**Labels:** feature, sim

- Pawn Shield Wall: 15% / 30% less damage with an orthogonally adjacent friend
- Knight Fork: also hits 1 / all other enemies on its knight squares
- Bishop Blessing: heals most wounded ally for 60% / 120% ATK, never above max HP
- Rook Fortress: 20% / 35% less damage
- Queen Pierce: hits the enemy directly behind the target for 50% / 100%

**Done when:** each ability has a unit test at 1★ (off), 2★ and 3★.
