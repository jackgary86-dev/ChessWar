# Art direction and style guide

**Milestone:** Art  
**Labels:** art, direction

Decide the game's look before any final assets are made. Everything else in the Art milestone depends on this.

- Pick a rendering style (e.g. flat vector, painted 2D, pixel art, or pre-rendered 3D) and show 2–3 mood boards
- Lock the palette as design tokens (Ivory side, Ebony side, portal, brass accent, UI neutrals, tier colors T1–T4)
- Typography: display face for the logo and headings, body face, mono face for notation and numbers
- Rules for readability: a piece must be recognizable at 28 px on a phone

**Done when:** `docs/art/STYLE_GUIDE.md` is merged with palette, fonts, sample piece and sample board square.
