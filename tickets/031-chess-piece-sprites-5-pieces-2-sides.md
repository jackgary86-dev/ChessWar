# Chess piece sprites: 5 pieces × 2 sides

**Milestone:** Art  
**Labels:** art, sprites

Final art for Pawn, Knight, Bishop, Rook and Queen in Ivory and Ebony.

- SVG (preferred) or PNG at 1×, 2×, 3×; transparent background; consistent base line and bounding box
- Readable at 28 px and crisp at 128 px
- Ivory and Ebony must both read clearly on both board colors
- Replace the Unicode glyphs in the renderer behind a single `pieceSprite(type, side)` function

**Done when:** all 10 sprites are in `assets/pieces/` and render in-game.
